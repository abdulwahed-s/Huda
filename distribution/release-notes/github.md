## What's new in 4.3.0

- Prayer times, notifications, and widgets are more reliable, with offline location verification.
- A refreshed prayer setup and timeline make it easier to review times and share a prayer calendar.
- Improved offline Quran reading and audio, including translations, tafsir, and selected ayah playback ranges.
- Hadith text is easier to read and can now be shared as an image.
- UI and UX improvements throughout the app.
