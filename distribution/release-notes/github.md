## What's new in 5.0.0

- Complete redesign: new themes (including pure black), adaptive layouts for tablets and foldables, configurable haptics, and better text scaling.
- Rebuilt prayer alerts that run natively on every platform and work offline: Adhan library with downloads and previews, per-prayer sound, volume, and style, and a delivery test.
- Iqamah windows: the current prayer and elapsed time stay visible across notifications, widgets, and countdowns.
- New Mushaf pages (Hafs, Tajweed, IndoPak) with faster page loading.
- iOS: full-length Adhan via AlarmKit, Siri and Shortcuts prayer times, native Sahur alarms.
- Android: Samsung Lock Screen widgets, Live Updates countdown, redesigned Adhan screen with headset Stop support.
- macOS: menu bar countdown, desktop widgets, Focus sessions.
- Windows: taskbar countdown and popup, native toasts, Focus sessions.
- Linux: GNOME prayer panel, native notifications, and Adhan playback.
- Guided onboarding, redesigned Zakat with live metal prices, and saving shared images to your gallery, Photos, or Downloads.
