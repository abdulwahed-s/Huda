Huda is a comprehensive Islamic companion app designed to support your daily worship and spiritual growth. It brings the tools Muslims need most into one clean, ad-free experience.

Features:

* Quran: complete Mushaf in authentic Uthmanic script, translations and Tafsir, audio recitations with offline playback, and smart reading-position tracking.

* Prayer Times & Adhan: accurate location-based prayer times with multiple calculation methods, Adhan notifications and countdown timers.

* Qibla Compass: precise Qibla direction with smooth animations and haptic feedback.

* Digital Tasbih: a persistent Dhikr counter with notes and tracking.

* Athkar & Duas: categorized daily supplications with audio pronunciation.

* Daily Checklist: track prayers and habits, build streaks, with Hijri calendar integration.

* Hadith & Islamic Library: curated books and Hadith collections with a built-in offline PDF reader.

* Home Screen Widgets: quick access to prayer times and core features.

* Miqaat Lock: optionally block selected distracting apps during prayer times or custom time slots. On Android, Huda requests Accessibility and display-over-other-apps access only when you choose to enable this feature.

* Multilingual & Themes: full RTL Arabic support, dynamic language switching, and Dark/Light themes.

Note: some features (such as content sync and the AI assistant) rely on a remote server to work. The FOSS edition asks for your permission before automatically sending crash details, device model, manufacturer, Android version and app version to Huda's Supabase service. You can change this choice later in Settings.
